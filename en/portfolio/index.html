Payment terms: 30% deposit upon signing the quote, balance due on delivery of your deliverables. Each payment can be split into 3 interest-free instalments with Klarna, by credit card.

Cancellation: once the quote is accepted, the 30% deposit paid at booking is retained by BUNKAIO and is non-refundable if you cancel.

The delivery timelines indicated on each package begin on the day of the shoot.

Usage rights to the delivered visuals are granted to you under the terms set out in the signed quote (uses, duration, territory).

Signing the quote: it is done online and covers your agreement to the quote and your image authorisations (a Yes or No answer is required for each use, the choice itself is free, never pre-ticked). Each authorisation can be withdrawn at any time.

One photographer, one eye

Aya Nascimento is a professional portrait photographer, a graduate of ETPA (BTS Photography, 2018). She is the photographer of BUNKAIO.

Specialised in fashion, product, corporate and event photography, she works with individuals, brands and companies, delivering premium high-definition images, carefully retouched.

A mobile studio in Occitanie

BUNKAIO is based in Occitanie and travels anywhere in France: the shoot takes place at your place, outdoors or, as an option, in the studio (+€60). Travel is free within 30 km of Montpellier, of Toulouse and in western Hérault. Elsewhere it is charged at €0.60 per km, round trip, on the distance exceeding those 30 km, rounded to the nearest €5, up to a long-distance flat rate capped at €350 (estimates: Nîmes ≈ €35, Carcassonne ≈ €70, Perpignan ≈ €80, Paris €350). The online quote calculates the amount from the session city; it is confirmed on your quote, before you sign anything.
